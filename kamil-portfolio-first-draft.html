Leadership

Operational rigor, human scale.

At the School of Visual Arts, I lead operations for the BFA Film department—supporting students, faculty, curriculum, and the practical systems that allow a creative program to do its best work.

My approach combines clear processes with close attention to the people inside them: building reliable structures, mentoring emerging filmmakers, and helping academic teams turn ambitious ideas into daily reality.
